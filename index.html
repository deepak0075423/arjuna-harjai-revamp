About

Born into music, shaped by tradition, driven by innovation.

Arjuna Harjai is a renowned composer, singer-songwriter, and music producer whose journey began at the tender age of two and a half, when his parents introduced him to the rich traditions of Hindustani classical music.

Now based in the UK, Arjuna has carved a distinguished career spanning Bollywood films, advertising, and independent music. With over 500 advertising jingles and a dozen feature films to his credit, his compositions have touched millions of hearts worldwide.

His work seamlessly blends the depth of classical Indian music with contemporary genres including pop, Sufi, ghazals, R&B, and soul, creating a signature sound that is both timeless and modern.
